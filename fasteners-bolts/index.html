Bolts

Page 40 of 56 - Fasteners

This section defines properties for structural fasteners modelled or referenced within the BIM environment. Fastener information supports connection design verification, fabrication coordination, and quality assurance without requiring every bolt, weld, or stud to be modelled as discrete geometry. Properties are grouped to align with IFC fastener and material property sets.

This section defines bolt-related properties used to describe mechanical fasteners within structural connections. The information captures bolt grade, geometry standards, head form, and certification data required for design coordination, fabrication, and quality verification.

Bolt properties are often shared across multiple connections; grade and standard references should be applied consistently and linked to steel material Psets with a _Bolt suffix. At design stage, nominal dimensions and grade are usually sufficient; at construction stage, certificates and verified product data complete the record.

These properties are typically applied to connection proxy elements or grouped fastener definitions rather than to individually modelled bolts.

Bolt properties are assigned to connection proxy elements (for example IfcBuildingElementProxy) or to IfcFastener occurrences, depending on the exchange strategy. Use a _Bolt suffix on extended material Psets (for example Pset_MaterialSteel_Bolt) so bolt data remains distinguishable from base steel material in the IFC file.

Bolt Certificate should use IfcDocumentReference where supported. Dimensional properties use IfcPositiveLengthMeasure with units via IfcUnitAssignment. Head Shape and standard references are IfcLabel.

Head ShapeCondition
Head shape refers to the geometric form of the bolt head, such as hexagonal, square, or countersunk, which determines how the bolt is installed, tightened, and interacts with tools and connected components. The head shape influences load distribution, accessibility during installation, and compatibility with fastening systems, making it an important attribute for both construction practicality and connection performance.

PitchCondition
Pitch refers to the distance between corresponding points on adjacent threads of a bolt, typically measured parallel to the bolt axis. It defines the spacing of the threads and directly influences how the bolt engages with a matching nut or threaded hole. The pitch affects tightening behaviour, load distribution, and resistance to loosening, with finer pitches providing greater adjustment precision and coarse pitches generally offering faster installation and improved performance in less controlled conditions.
CustomPset_StructuralConnectionBolts → PitchIfcReal2.5 [mm]
note

Bolt Grade uses project notation (for example, 8.8 or 10.9). Bolt Material Standard and Bolt Shape Standard reference the governing product standard (for example, EN ISO 4014). Nominal Head Diameter, Head Depth, and Bolt Length are length measures, typically millimetres. Bolt Certificate links to the mill or test certificate for the installed batch; at early construction only Bolt Length or diameter may be known until submittals are confirmed.
