Steel - Construction stage

Page 12 of 56 - Materials

This section defines how material information is captured within the BIM model using structured property sets. Material data provides the physical, mechanical, and performance characteristics required for analysis, fabrication, quality assurance, and lifecycle asset management. These properties should be applied consistently to ensure that material behaviour is correctly represented across all relevant structural elements.

This section defines the required material properties for structural steel at the construction stage. The information captures verified product data, including mill test results, certification records, and traceability information associated with delivered steel components.

These properties support quality assurance, compliance verification, and construction-stage validation of material performance against the design specification and applicable standards. Compared to the design stage, the focus shifts from assumed characteristic values to actual measured and certified properties of supplied materials.

Manufacturer declarations, mill test certificates, and batch-specific test results should be linked to specific deliveries or components where possible to support traceability through fabrication, installation, and future inspection.

The information is primarily mapped to IFC material property sets such as Pset_MaterialSteel, Pset_MaterialMechanical, Pset_ElementComponentCommon, and Pset_ManufacturerTypeInformation.

Construction-stage properties are assigned through IfcRelAssociates to material or component entities, commonly including Pset_ManufacturerTypeInformation. Performance Certificate should use IfcDocumentReference where supported to preserve a machine-readable link to the source file.

Where IfcDocumentReference is not available on export, IfcLabel may store a document identifier or path. Mechanical test values (Tensile Strength, Yield Point, Elongation) should use the typed measures indicated in the Definition column, with IfcReal only as a documented fallback per project exchange rules.

ElongationNecessary
Mill test elongation is the measured percentage increase in gauge length of a steel specimen before fracture during tensile testing. It is an indicator of ductility and deformation capacity, verifying that the steel meets minimum ductility requirements specified in relevant standards.
CustomPset_SteelElementGeneral → MillElongationIfcReal12 [%]
Tensile StrengthNecessary
Mill test tensile strength is the maximum stress the steel sample sustains during the mill’s tensile test before breaking. This value confirms that the steel achieves or exceeds its specified ultimate tensile strength as required by the applicable material standard.
CustomPset_SteelElementGeneral → MillTensileStrengthIfcReal392 [MPa]
Yield PointNecessary
Mill test yield point is the stress at which the steel transitions from elastic to plastic behaviour as determined in mill testing. It verifies the characteristic yield strength of the steel batch and ensures that installed material meets the specified structural grade requirements.
CustomPset_SteelElementGeneral → MillYieldPointIfcReal534 [MPa]
note

Performance Certificate should reference the mill test certificate or EN 10204 document for the delivered batch, not a generic material type record. Corrosion Protection is typically IfcLabel describing the applied system or coating reference.

Tensile Strength, Yield Point, and Elongation record tested values from the certificate; they are verification data and sit alongside, not in place of, design-stage characteristic values in Pset_MaterialMechanical.
