Planar profiles - Hollowcore slab

Page 37 of 56 - Profiles

This section defines geometric parameters for planar structural elements and decking systems within the BIM model. These parameters describe through-thickness geometry, void layouts, rib configurations, and composite deck profiles used to represent slabs, floors, and similar elements for coordination, take-off, and documentation.

This section defines geometric parameters for precast hollowcore slab units. The parameters capture overall thickness, void dimensions, spacing, and voided area proportion for standard plank profiles.

These values support structural coordination, erection planning, and verification that the modelled unit matches the manufacturer's catalogue section.

Parameters are custom properties on the precast slab type or occurrence. Length fields use IfcPositiveLengthMeasure; Voided Area Percentage uses IfcRatio or IfcNormalisedRatioMeasure where supported. Each distinct plank designation should be a separate IfcTypeObject with its own property set.

Voided Area PercentageNecessary
Voided area percentage is the proportion of the total cross‑sectional area of a slab that is occupied by voids, expressed as a percentage of the overall slab area. It is used to quantify the reduction in concrete volume, influencing the element’s self‑weight, material efficiency, and structural performance, particularly with respect to stiffness and load‑carrying capacity.
